# Ganchos

The first two seconds decide most of the outcome, so this is where the thinking goes.

## Why templates stopped working

"Eu parei de fazer X e Y aconteceu." "Ninguém tá falando sobre isso." "Isso vai mudar a forma como você..." These were strong once. Now the audience recognizes the shape before the sentence finishes and scrolls — and because everyone in the niche is using the same five openers, a template hook makes an account sound like every other account in the feed.

What survives is the **mechanism** underneath. The mechanisms below are structural; they do not wear out, because they can be filled with something specific each time. Specificity is the active ingredient: "seu deploy quebrou às 3 da manhã" works because it is about something, and no template produces it.

## The mechanisms

### 1. Abrir um loop
State something incomplete and withhold the resolution. The gap creates the pull.

> "Esse erro aqui custou dezoito mil reais e levou três dias pra alguém achar."

Works because the viewer needs to know what the error was. **Close the loop in the video**, or the payoff moves to the comments and the video feels like bait.

### 2. Cravar uma posição
Say something a reasonable person could dispute. Disagreement generates comments, and comments generate reach.

> "Testar não previne bug. Previne outra coisa, e quase ninguém sabe o quê."

Only use this when the user can actually defend it. Handing someone a fight they do not believe in ends badly in the replies.

### 3. Nomear um inimigo
Name the thing the audience already resents. Recognition is instant and it puts you on their side.

> "A reunião que podia ser um email agora virou um dashboard que ninguém abre."

The enemy should be a situation or a practice, not a person or a competitor by name.

### 4. Resultado antes do método
Show the finished thing first. The viewer stays to find out how.

> "Isso aqui tá em produção há duas semanas. Eu escrevi zero linha de CSS."

The strongest mechanism when there is something to show, and the most reliably replicable — it costs one edit decision, not a better idea.

### 5. Quebrar uma expectativa
Set up the obvious, then break it in the same breath.

> "Passei seis meses otimizando isso. A solução foi apagar o arquivo."

### 6. Começar no meio da ação
No setup, no introduction. Open inside something already happening.

> [tela já mostrando o erro] "...e é aqui que todo mundo desiste."

Removes exactly the part people scroll past. Works especially well for faceless and screen-recording formats.

### 7. Endereçar direto
Name the specific person this is for. Everyone else scrolls, which is fine — they were never converting.

> "Se você tem um SaaS que fatura menos de dez mil por mês, esse número aqui é o seu problema."

Narrower than it looks and that is the point: precision beats reach when the goal is anything but views.

## How to build one

1. **Find the sharpest specific thing you have** — a number, a moment, a failure, a visible result. The hook is almost always already in the material.
2. **Pick the mechanism that fits it.** A number wants position or expectation-break. A visible result wants result-before-method. A shared frustration wants the enemy.
3. **Cut it to a breath.** Under ~12 words for spoken, under ~8 for on-screen text. If it needs a comma to survive, it is two sentences or it is too long.
4. **Read it out loud.** Written-sounding hooks die instantly. If you would not say it to a friend, rewrite it.
5. **Check it can be scrolled past.** Be honest. If the answer is "yes, easily", go back to step 1 — you probably reached for a phrasing when you needed a better specific.

## Three options, three mechanisms

Always give the user three, and make them differ in mechanism rather than in wording. Three phrasings of the same idea is one option wearing three shirts. Label each one so the user can judge it:

```
GANCHO A [resultado antes do método]
"Esse site inteiro foi feito em quarenta minutos. Olha o commit."

GANCHO B [cravar uma posição]
"Boilerplate não te economiza tempo. Te economiza decisão, que é outra coisa."

GANCHO C [nomear um inimigo]
"Você não tá lento. Sua stack tem catorze passos pra fazer um botão."
```

## The first three seconds are not only words

The hook is the frame, the first sound and the first on-screen text together. A mediocre line over a strong opening image outperforms a clever line over a slow one. When the format is faceless, the visual usually *is* the hook — so specify it with the same care as the sentence, not as an afterthought.
